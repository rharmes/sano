## Notes for whoever reviews

- Romanization in the app is informal and aimed at English-speaking pronunciation, not IAST — "what would you type in a text message" is the right standard.
- The app drills these daily, so anything wrong here gets memorized wrong. Better to cut a doubtful item than keep it.
