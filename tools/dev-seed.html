SR-05 adaptive daily loop

0b · Review debt throttles new words (SR-05)

Sets up a big backlog of due reviews on top of a current unit that still has new words. Because review debt is high, the daily loop serves all review and 0 new words — the home button reads “… · N review” with no “new,” so a growing vocabulary can never outrun its reviews. Clear the backlog and new words return.
