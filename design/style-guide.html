Color & theme

Every color is a CSS custom property declared on html in css/sano.css, with a dark override inside @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark). In the app there is no theme toggle — the OS decides; the switch up top re-themes only this guide. Always edit both blocks together. Chips below are rendered with the live token, so they preview the active theme; both hex values are listed for reference.

Typography

Two self-hosted families (no external requests). Neuton (serif, 700 only) is the display voice — the wordmark, headings, and every Nepali phrase. Lato (300 / 400 / 700, plus 300 / 400 italic) carries all UI and body copy; italic Lato is the house style for Romanized pronunciations. Sizes are rem on a 62.5% base, so 1rem = 10px (body 2.3rem = 23px).

Iconography

Icons are an inline SVG sprite of Material Icons symbols (#i-*) defined once in index.html, used via <use href="#i-name">. Wrap each in <span class="icon"> (or a chromeless <button class="icon"> for actions): the wrapper's font-size sets glyph size and color sets fill (currentColor). No icon font, no network request.

Sano the mascot

A paper-cut mouse built from layered SVG polygons on a viewBox="30 10 160 174" (widened from the original to give the tilted ears headroom). Fills are theme tokens via .f-indigo / .f-crimson / .f-petal / .f-cream / .f-cream-deep / .f-ink and the .s-whisker stroke. The indigo backing is split into separate head and body outlines so the head can tilt independently, and the moving parts live in named groups (.sano-head / .sano-tail / .sano-eyes / .sano-ear / .sano-nose) that the idle loops target. The footer uses a head-only crop — which carries a bolder indigo outline (tapered at the bottom to follow the chin) plus the full mascot's whiskers. Sano renders flat: shapes are layered directly with no drop-shadow or grain.

Buttons & inputs

The base layer is barebones.css (38px controls, --accent mapped from crimson). These are the live shared classes — .btn-primary (with the .indigo advance variant), .btn-secondary for a paired ghost button, .icon-btn, and .field — used across the home CTA, lesson flow, login panel, reminder modal, and onboarding. Primary buttons are a softer, rounder shape than barebones: 1rem radius, mixed-case 700 Lato, crimson fill that deepens on hover. Icon buttons drop all chrome and tint with currentColor. (Onboarding adds two documented variants in css/sano.css: tappable choice speech-bubbles and the in-bubble fill-in-the-blank .onboard-input.) Audio adds .audio-btn, the icon-only tap-to-play pronunciation speaker (SR-02).

Panels & cards

Floating surfaces use the shared .panel class — page background, a --border-color hairline, 1rem radius, a soft drop-shadow. The login panel is .panel plus positioning; the reminder modal is a real <dialog> (.panel.reminder-card) — a heavier variant (wider, more padding, a stronger shadow to lift it off the dimmed backdrop) that opens with Sano's head and a speech bubble so the daily-reminder ask is in his voice, and pops in on open (see Motion). Inset rows are divided with --border-color-softer.

Speech bubbles

Onboarding

First-run onboarding: Sano speaks from the left with a head-only avatar beside each of its bubbles; the learner's replies sit on the right with no avatar. Each bubble leads with Romanized Nepali in Neuton 700, English underneath in italic Lato, and a tail points back toward the speaker. (The top-of-path intro reuses these same bubbles, but with one full-size Sano beside the pair instead of per-line heads.) Re-keyed onto the canonical tokens above.

Tapaiko naam ke ho?

What is your name?

Mero naam Aastha ho.

My name is Aastha.

Conversations

Story dialogues use a different bubble: every speaker — Sano or a companion — sits on the left behind their own head; lines are Romanized-only in Lato (no English subtitle — tap an underlined word for its gloss), with a left tail pointing at the head. Narrator lines run full-width with no bubble, in italic. Each line auto-plays its audio (the inline speaker icon). Mirrors the dialogue player (#dialogue-thread + js/gloss.js).

Tala kasaile malai jiskyaairaheko cha! Sun — taadhaa jaau!

Khochle bistaarai jawaaf dinchha: taadhaa jaau… jaau…

Tyo ta pratidhwani ho. Khochle timrai aawaaj dohoryaaunchha.

T13: when a review exercise is asked in a companion's voice, that companion's head (CHARACTER_HEADS) sits centered above the prompt in a .voice-chip (4.4rem, in-flow so it can never overlap the word). Sano-voiced exercises and bundled match grids show no chip. Same paper-cut head art as the dialogue player, so it follows both themes.

Motion

Animation is gentle and purposeful: staggered top-to-bottom reveals on the lesson path, a pop-in + head-wobble + tail-wag flourish on lesson completion, a matching celebration on the onboarding finish (Sano pops and wobbles with a burst ring and a few paper-cut confetti flecks), a pop-in entrance on the reminder <dialog> as it opens, and a set of slow, periodic idle motions that keep Sano feeling alive without distracting (most of each cycle is at rest; periods are de-synced so motions don't all fire at once). Wherever Sano appears it runs these idle loops — the full mascot does all five (tail wag, head tilt, eye blink, ear wiggle, nose wiggle); the footer head crop runs only the three that read at thumbnail size (blink, ear, nose). Every Sano on this page is idling right now.

  • Always honor prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — every animated rule needs an opt-out (see the block at the bottom of css/sano.css).
  • Idle loops are long and quiet. 4–22s periods with a short active window read as "alive," not "busy."
  • Celebrate completion, not every tap. The big motion budget is spent on the two "win" moments — the lesson-complete screen and the onboarding finish — which share the same pop / burst / confetti vocabulary.

Design decisions

  • Brand: “Pennant & Paper-cut.” Softened Nepal-flag crimson + indigo on warm paper stock, with a paper-cut mouse named Sano whose centered head is the favicon and app icon (see design/icons.html).
  • Tokens are the contract. All theme values live as CSS variables at the top of css/sano.css — a light block and a dark @media block. Change both; never hard-code a hex in a component.
  • Dark mode is automatic. Driven entirely by prefers-color-scheme; dark is midnight card stock with colors lifted one step for contrast. No manual toggle in the app — the switch atop this guide just previews the two themes.
  • No build step, no runtime network. Plain HTML/CSS/JS. Fonts are self-hosted woff2 (Neuton, Lato); icons are an inline SVG sprite. The only fetches are same-origin calls to api/.
  • 1rem = 10px. Base font-size is 62.5% — read every rem as "tenths of the value × 10px."
  • Buttons round, not boxy. Primary actions are 1rem-radius crimson with mixed-case 700 Lato — softer than the barebones default they override.
  • Two type voices. Neuton 700 for display + all Nepali; Lato for everything else, italic for Romanized pronunciation.
  • Design files live in design/. Committed but excluded from the deploy rsync — nothing here ships to the live site. This guide and its companion pages (the animation tuner, character explorations, and the icon set) all live here.
