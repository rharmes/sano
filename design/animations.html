Idle animations

Tail wag

Tail pivots at its base, four wags back and forth.

Head tilt

Head leans left, holds, then straightens. Duration includes the 3s hold.

Eye blink

Eyes briefly squash to a horizontal slit and reopen.

Ear wiggle

Both ears twitch outward (mirrored via a CSS variable), then settle.

Nose wiggle

Nose and whiskers twitch together — small rotation + lateral nudge.

Action animations

Head shake

Head pivots at the neck — three swings, then a small follow-through.

Body wiggle

Whole body sways at the feet — subtle, sub-2° rotation.

Excited jump

Big hop, soft squash on landing, a smaller second hop.

Little bow

Head tips forward (translateY + scaleY squish), holds, returns.
