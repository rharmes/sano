English gloss alignment (T59)

Every English exercise prompt, with the Nepali word each of its words hints at when tapped. The alignment is machine-made (tools/build-en-glosses.mjs) and deliberately timid — it leaves a word plain rather than guess, so the gaps in red are missing hints, not wrong ones. Fix a word by picking its Nepali from the dropdown. The line above each row is the lesson's own rendering. Grouping works both ways: give two neighbouring English words the same hint and they become one span under one underline (“go ahead” → bhannus), and each dropdown also offers multi-word Nepali phrases for the reverse case, where one English word is a whole phrase (“sorry” → maaf garnuhos). Parentheticals and “/” are greyed out and skipped — “(formal)” describes register and “/” separates two glosses; neither has a Nepali word behind it, so neither gets an underline. Saves to design/en-gloss-review.json; nothing here edits js/data.js or ships.
